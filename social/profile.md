## Voice

<!-- How you sound. Swap in your own; these are starting defaults. -->
- Plain, direct, first person. Short sentences.
- No hype words ("game-changer", "excited to announce", "unlock").
- At most two hashtags, and only where the platform uses them (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Threads).
- Emoji: sparingly, never more than one per post.

## Rules

- Only state facts that are in the topic notes or in a source linked from the draft's notes section.
- No prices, dates, or promises unless the topic notes give them.
- Never mention other people or businesses by name unless the notes do.
