# Topics

Add a topic under **Inbox**, one bullet each, with any notes, facts, links or a preferred posting time.
The drafting routine picks up everything in the Inbox, opens a PR of drafts, and moves the bullet to **Drafted**.

Optional tags at the end of a bullet:
- `[when: 2026-10-09 09:00 +02:00]` publish no earlier than this
- `[only: linkedin, x]` draft just for these platforms
- `[image_url: https://…]` public image link. Instagram and TikTok need an image or a video, and Facebook can use one.
- `[video_url: https://…]` public video link. YouTube needs one, and Instagram, TikTok and Facebook can use one.
- `[youtube_title: …]` title for the YouTube video

A Google Drive or Dropbox share link counts as a public link if your Google Drive or Dropbox is linked in Metricool.
